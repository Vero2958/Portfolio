**TRAITS** is a train ticketing system, where travellers can plan their train journeys, book and buy tickets, and access real-time updates about train schedules, routes, and availability.

**Purpose**: Scenario that describes the use of an online train ticketing system used by a representative traveller.  
**Individual**: Passenger A.  
**Equipment**: Any device with an Internet connection.  
**Scenario**:  
   1. Passenger A chooses place of origin and destination, date and time of arrival/departure.  
   2. Passenger A clicks on Search Connection or Book Ticket.  
   3. Both choices eventually lead to the shop, where Passenger A can specify the number of passengers, children, seniors, if there are any dogs or bicycles by clicking on Change.  
   4. Passenger A clicks on Find Services to indicate what kind of ticket they need (one-way ticket and day tickets, Weekly and monthly tickets, Seat reservation).  
   5. Passenger A clicks on one-way ticket and day tickets.  
   6. The system displays a list of possible connections with their times and prices, as well as if the journey is direct or includes changes.  
   7. Passenger A clicks on the chosen connection.  
   8. The system displays fare categories and products.  
   9. Passenger A can either select a different option or carry on with the default one.  
   10. Passenger A decides for the default option and clicks on Continue with given price.  
   11. The system requires the passenger to insert the personal information, with fields for First name and Last name.  
   12. Passenger A fills in the form and clicks on Continue.  
   13. The system requires a passenger to specify a method of payment and a passenger's email, and to accept Terms and Conditions.  
   14. Passenger A fills in the required fields and clicks on *price* pay now.  
   15. The system redirects to the payment webpage.  
   16. Passenger A fills in the credit card information and clicks on Buy.  
   17. The system shows the confirmation page.  
   18. Passenger A receives an email with the link to the downloadable e-ticket and his travel information.
